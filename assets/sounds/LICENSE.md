# City sounds — where each one comes from

Every file here is cut from a recording released under **CC0 1.0** (public
domain dedication), checked on the recording's own page on 2026-10-20. No
attribution is required; it is given anyway. The Freesound files are cut from
the site's high-quality previews, not the originals.

`steps/<surface>-<n>.ogg` are single footsteps, peak-normalised, mono.
`ambience/<bed>.ogg` are 30-second seamless loops, loudness-normalised to one
level (−20 LUFS) so the mix in `systems::ambience` decides how loud each is.
How they were cut is in `docs/notes/city-soundscape.md`.

## Crows

`crows/caw-<n>.ogg` are single caws, mono, peak-normalised to −1.5 dBFS, with a
15 ms fade in and an 80 ms fade out; `systems::crows` plays them. Cut at the
quiet between calls (each caw is 18–20 dB over the recording's background),
and checked as spectrograms — a twelfth take was another bird and was dropped.

| Files | Recording | Author |
|---|---|---|
| `caw-1`–`caw-4` | Call of a carrion crow (Germany) | [cfrooos](https://freesound.org/people/cfrooos/sounds/181088/) |
| `caw-5`–`caw-11` | Crows sounding the alarm at a fox in a garden | [richwise](https://freesound.org/people/richwise/sounds/521019/) |

## Water

`water/` is what `systems::splashes` plays (`docs/notes/water-splashes.md`):
single splashes and strokes peak-normalised to −1.5 dBFS with a 5 ms fade in
and a 150 ms fade out, and two 27–30 s loops at −20 LUFS crossfaded
end-into-start over 3 s. All mono Ogg Vorbis, 48 kHz, cut from the HQ
previews; every recording checked as CC0 on its own page on 2026-11-16. Cut by
onset detection and checked as waveform pictures, spectra (no mains hum, no
sustained voice) and loop seams — not by ear.
