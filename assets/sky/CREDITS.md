# Sky data — credits

## bright_stars.bin

Compact naked-eye star catalog (right ascension, declination, visual magnitude,
B−V color index per star; ~9k stars to magnitude 6.5). Generated by
`tools/gen_stars.py`; embedded into the client at compile time and rendered as
the night-sky star field (`setup_stars` in `crates/cartopolis/src/main.rs`).

- **Source:** HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash —
  https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database — a compilation of the Hipparcos,
  Yale Bright Star, and Gliese catalogs.
