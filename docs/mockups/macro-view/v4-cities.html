Cities with detail from a distance new

Everything on this sheet comes from the OSM tiles the client already streams for the street view (Shortbread): building footprints with their heights, land use, streets by rank, stations and district names. From far off the client draws a city today as one grey blob. Here each of these elements is kept for longer as you climb, each drawn in the painted palette of v3, and they give way one after another rather than all at once.

Buildings, with shadows

Footprints from the buildings layer, roofs in Dutch tile and slate tones. Each casts a shadow from its own height and the real sun (30 Oct, noon: 24° high).

Land use tells the city apart

Housing warm grey, industry and ports blue-grey, shops and offices mauve-grey, parks and allotments darker green, water navy.

Streets by rank

Motorways gold, main roads cream with a dark edge, local streets fine and pale. Railways dashed.

Names that belong to the place

Districts from place_labels in small capitals, stations from public_transport, cities as banners.

Which element, from which height

  • Building footprints and shadows: up to about 60 km.
  • Local streets: up to 20 km. Main roads to 150 km, motorways throughout.
  • District names: neighbourhoods to 20 km, larger districts to 150 km.
  • Stations: named to 20 km, an icon to 60 km.
  • Land-use colours and the lifted built-up edge: from 40 km to the strategy map, where WorldCover takes over.

Notes

  • No new data: every element is a layer the client already loads at street level. Only the zoom at which it stops is new.
  • Roof tones vary per building by a hash of its outline. OSM has no roof colours for most buildings. The client's 3DBAG roofs could replace this in the Netherlands.
  • Shadows use the tile's height. 3DBAG heights would be more precise where the client has them.
