From the street to the globe new

v3 after review: the painted map now uses colours closer to today's client (muted greens, the same navy sea, grey towns), so the two blend instead of switching. Clouds are a setting, and the globe gets a night side. The painted look follows distance from the camera, not camera height. What is near you always keeps today's styling, even in a tilted view at 40 km. Only the ground far off, towards the horizon, turns painted.

1What fades in and out, by altitude

The 3D city, the painted ground, banners and the hex grid are new. For the painted ground the axis is the distance from the camera to the ground, which equals the height only when looking straight down. Only the bottom row, globe and space, exists today.

2One zoom out over Utrecht new

The 3, 15 and 40 km frames are captures of today's client on mac-1 (real GPU), with the proposed layers drawn over them. Banners on the two tilted frames are placed by hand. Every frame from 40 km up is drawn from data, framed to match the client's camera.

5The night side new

The same globe on 30 October at 17:30 UTC. The sun is over the Americas and night has fallen over Europe. The terminator is computed from the sun's position, with about 12° of twilight. City lights come from Natural Earth's urban areas and towns, and show only on the dark side.

8,000 km · noon
8,000 km · 17:30 UTC

What it is made of

  • No new map data. Up to about 50 km the painted ground is a second style of the OSM tiles the client already streams. Above that, it is the WorldCover landcover the globe already uses.
  • Near the camera nothing changes. City banners appear from 2 km.
  • The fades ride on distance, as the sky does on altitude today, so a fast zoom never jumps.

Changed since v2

  • Palette moved towards the client's: grass #6f8a55, farmland #979262, forest #3e5a3c, towns #8a817a, sea navy. The tree marks and wave lines are fainter.
  • Clouds: a setting, off by default from above.
  • Night side with city lights, following the real sun.
  • The v1 sheets are re-rendered in the new palette.
