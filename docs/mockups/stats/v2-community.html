EA community's stats

  • In Moderation, beside Reports and Settings new, for the owner and the moderators. Members do not see it; a server admin sees every community's numbers in Admin › Stats.
  • Counts, never names. There is no "most active members" list and no per-person column: a moderator learns how much a topic is used, not who uses it. Who is a member was already theirs to see.
  • Fewer than 5: a topic where fewer than 5 different people wrote shows that instead of numbers, so a small topic cannot be read as one person's activity.
  • Active member means wrote in a topic, joined a call, or opened the community in the 30 days — the same sessions Admin › Stats keeps, cut to this community.
  • Messages are counted per topic as the server relays them; topics are encrypted, so this is the only thing it can count.
  • Fediverse: followers per server and the replies and boosts that came in, from the public half of the community.

✓Decided on v1 (2026-10-18)

  • Moderators see their community's numbers — this sheet.
  • No public numbers on about.cartopolis.org; the "Public numbers" switch on v1's sidebar goes.
  • No page of a person's own sessions.
  • Sessions are kept per account for 90 days, so "who comes back", "active in 30 days" and the funnel stay.
  • Global Privacy Control is not honoured: nothing counted identifies anyone, and every browser is counted the same.
