Chat over the map: thirty seconds on screen, a day on the server

A proposal for the chat overlay (systems::chat_overlay). It changes three things. Here and Everyone stop being an archive: a line is on the map for thirty seconds and in the Community thread for a day, then it is deleted, on the server too. The overlay becomes one quiet column with the composer under it. And voice moves out of ☰ ▸ Chat into the chat itself. Private chats keep their own rules. Only how a new private message is announced over the map changes. Marked NEW where it does not exist in the app today.

v2, 2026-10-17. The action buttons stay centred along the bottom, as in the app. Nothing is pinned to the window's right edge, where every panel opens. The private notice goes under the Community | World switch at the top, and section 1b shows the screen with a panel open. The list is called Community, as the app has called it since 2026-10-16.

Public chat is a conversation

Here and Everyone keep one day, on the server and on the device. Sign-in replays only that day. Today the server keeps Everyone forever and replays the last 50 lines.

The map shows the last 30 seconds

A line holds for 25 s and fades over 5. Lines replayed at sign-in are never drawn over the map, including while you write. They are in Community.

Private stays out of the public column

A 🔒 line is never mixed into Here and Everyone over the map. It comes as one notice, under the Community | World switch it belongs to, and leaves after 30 s.

Voice is where you talk

A mic disc beside the chat disc, a mic in the composer when the field is empty, a badge over whoever is speaking, and the mode is one tap away.

The column NEW

  • One glass card, fixed width, lines wrapping inside it. It replaces a chip_frame per line. Type at the body size, not the heading size.
  • The scope is an icon, not a prefix: a pin for Here, a globe in the public tone for Everyone. [Global] goes away.
  • A colour per person, from the same six tones as their disc in Community, so a name in the column and a disc in the list look like one person. Your own lines are “You” in cream, body dimmed.
  • 30 seconds: hold 25 s, fade 5 s (today 10 + 4), oldest line first. At most four lines. HOLD_SECS, FADE_SECS.

Over heads

  • Speech bubble EXISTS: a Here line over the person who said it. It is restyled to the column's glass and takes their name colour.
  • Voice badge NEW: bars and a name over whoever is audible. VoiceState::speakers is keyed by sender_id, which is RemoteAvatar::sim_id, so it finds its head the way a bubble does, with no wire change.

A private message NEW

  • One notice, at the top, under the Community | World switch whose count it just raised. Lock, name, the first line, Reply. Reply opens that chat in Community. The bar along the bottom is its 30 seconds running out.
  • A second message from the same chat updates the notice (“3 new”) rather than stacking.

The mic disc NEW

  • Beside the chat disc, while signed in. Its face shows the mode: mic-off, armed, live. The caret opens the voice popover (section 3).
  • The row stays centred along the bottom, as it is today, with one disc more.

Centred on the map, not the window NEW

  • Today the action buttons are centred on the window (dock.rs anchors them CENTER_BOTTOM of the viewport), and so is the switch at the top. With a panel open on a narrow window, the right end of the row already slides under the panel.
  • Centred on the map area instead, the space the panels leave, which is the rect place_pins already culls its pins against, they stay clear at any width.
  • The private notice hangs from the switch, so it moves with it.
  • On a phone a panel is the whole screen and the buttons hide, as today.

Why not the right-hand corner

  • v1 put the action buttons and the voice popover bottom right and the notice top right. Every panel opens on the right, so all three would have been behind the first panel opened.

One column, bottom left NEW

  • The composer is the column's bottom edge, as in most games' chat. Opening it doesn't move what you were reading, and the line you reply to sits right above the field. Its width is the column's, so it cannot run off the window.
  • On a phone it is full width above the keyboard (section 4), and stack_bottoms keeps doing that job.

While writing, five minutes, never older NEW

  • Up to eight lines that arrived during this session in the last five minutes. The infinite window goes away, so a replayed line can never show here.
  • Anything older is one tap away: Open in Community goes to the Here or Everyone thread, whichever scope is selected.
  • Names take taps, as today (person_menu), plus Mute voice: quiet for you only, not a block.

The bar

  • Here | Everyone, the names Community already uses, as two segments instead of a chip that cycles. Private chats are no longer in it. They are answered in Community or from the notice's Reply. NEW
  • Shout is a toggle in the field, shown only for Here. EXISTS, moved.

  • The scope row says what this chat is: public, how far it reaches, and that it is gone after a day. For Everyone it reads “everyone on this server”. NEW

The disc does the mode's main action NEW

  • Off (mic-off face): tap opens the popover, the first time with the permission ask.
  • Hold to talk: press and hold to talk, as V does. The disc turns orange and rings while frames go out (VoiceState::transmitting).
  • Open mic: tap mutes and unmutes. The face carries a thin teal level ring so an open microphone is never invisible.
  • The caret always opens the popover. Right-click or a long press on the disc does too.

The popover NEW, from parts that exist

  • Off / Hold to talk / Open mic, the meter, the error line and “Hearing N people” are chat::voice_row today, moved here from ☰ ▸ Chat.
  • New: the list of who you hear with their distance, from VoiceState::speakers and distance_m, and mute per person. Muting is local, nothing on the wire, and is the same toggle as the name menu's Mute voice.
  • The Voices slider is AppPrefs::voice_volume, which Settings already has.
  • The subtitle states what voice is: public, 50 m (SAY_RADIUS_M), and not stored anywhere, which is already true.

Who is talking

  • On screen: the badge over their head. Your own badge is orange, so you see what others see while you hold V.
  • Off screen: a talking row in the column, with distance and direction, for as long as they talk. It is the same rule the transcript already follows for Here lines from people out of view.

6To decide

Five minutes while writing, or 30 s throughout?

Drawn: 30 s at rest, five minutes of this session while the composer is open, never anything replayed. That lets you answer a line from a minute ago without leaving the map. Strictly 30 s is simpler, and then “Open in Community” is the only way back.

Does a private notice show the text?

Drawn: name and first line, as a phone notification does. The private alternative is “Marit sent you a message” with no text, since this is drawn over the map where others may see the screen.

Private chats out of the composer?

Drawn: yes. The overlay composer is Here and Everyone only, and a private chat is answered in Community or from the notice's Reply. Today the scope chip cycles into private chats too.

Is the mic disc always in the dock?

Drawn: always, while signed in, because discoverability is today's problem. The alternative is to show it only once voice has been switched on (from the composer's mic or the popover), which keeps the dock at five on a phone.
