Chat over the map: one chat at a time, and a call that joins it

v3, 2026-11-14. The map's chat on a phone today is a wall of large bubbles over the whole screen, every run headed by a name and a chat name, under and over the map's buttons, with a composer whose hint names a different chat from the one on screen. This proposal replaces the transcript and the composer pill with one sheet: a row of chats at its top, the open chat's lines in a compact list, the field at its foot. Talking and video are a button in that row, and a call that is on becomes a strip in the same sheet, so the conversation goes on under it. Marked NEW where it does not exist in the app today, HAS where it does and only moves.

One chat on screen

The sheet shows one chat, chosen in its row of tabs. No line carries a chat name, because the tab says it. Today Here and Everyone are merged, and each run says "· Everyone".

The map keeps its half

Open, the sheet takes the bottom of the screen and nothing above it. The map's buttons step aside while you write. Bubbles stay over heads, short, in the world that is still visible.

Lines, not posters

Body 14.5 pt, a 26 pt face and the name once per run, your own lines in a quiet accent on the right. About three times as many lines fit as today, on a solid sheet you can read over any building.

A call is part of the chat

Talk nearby in Here, an encrypted call in a private chat: one button in the tab row. In a call the faces or tiles go above the lines; the chat stays open. Full screen is one tap, not the default.

3What it changes, and what stays

Goes

  • The merged transcript of every chat, and its "· Everyone" headers.
  • The composer pill and its scope chip that cycles; the tab is the scope.
  • "Shout" as a word in the field: it is an item under +.
  • The chat drawn over the left column and the switch.

Stays

  • Balloons over heads, painted and never tappable (chat-without-a-panel.md).
  • Clearing the keyboard before it exists (onscreen_keyboard::expected_height).
  • The messenger in Community, the chat's World view, its points.
  • Calls stay encrypted and forwarded by the simulator; proximity voice stays public. No wire change is needed.

Open

  • Which tabs: Here and Everyone always, plus private chats with something new in the last hour? Or only the ones you pin?
  • Does Everyone belong on the map at all, or only in Community?
  • A call from the map: start it in the strip (E), or ring as today and fold?
  • Talk nearby: open mic with mute (D), or push-to-talk only?
