One top bar for the content pages and the app

cartopolis.org has two top bars today: the content pages (/nl/…, drawn by src/cms/frame.tsx) and the app (/chat, /world, /admin, chat.tsx topBar). They look different, link differently, and neither leads to everything. The proposal: one bar, the same on every page, with the app's places and the site's pages side by side, and what is particular to the app (where you are in a community) on a thin second line under it, only inside the app.

The button holds the site's pages, Beheer, language, theme and the account: the same things as the wide bar, in the same order.

Where the site's links come from new

A page chooses to be in the menu, and its place there, as part of the page: a column in Admin › Content › Pages and a field the MCP put_page takes. The menu's label is the page's own title, or a short $t('nav') text when the title is too long for a bar ("Praten en samenwerken op de kaart…" is not a menu word). home is never in it: the mark is home.
