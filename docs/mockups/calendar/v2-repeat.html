Calendar v2: meetings that repeat

Decided on v1 (2026-10-19): no copying plans to the phone's own calendar, no reminders while the app is closed, meet-ups keep only "I'm going", and each big community gets the "only the plans I answered" switch. Repeating meetings are in the first version. This sheet adds them. Only meetings repeat; a meet-up stays a single place and time. Everything here is new.

1 A repeat is part of the plan

One plan with a rule ("every Thursday until 17 December"), not a pile of copies. Everyone's device works out the dates from the rule.

2 You answer one date

Coming this Thursday says nothing about next Thursday. A switch answers every date at once, for the regulars.

3 The planner can skip or stop

"Not on 29 October" and "stop after this one" change the plan for everybody. Both are said in the chat, so nobody misses it.

4 Older apps still see a meeting

An app from before this shows the first date as an ordinary plan. Nobody gets a broken message; they just miss the later dates until they update.

IPlanning it

  • The chat's Plan a meeting form, as it is today, plus Repeats: never, every week, every 2 weeks, or every month.
  • Every month means the same weekday of the month ("every 4th Thursday"), which is how neighbourhood meetings are usually set. A date that doesn't exist in a month (a 5th Thursday) is skipped.
  • Until steps by a week and says how many meetings that makes. No end is allowed.

In an app from before this change, the card shows only "Buurtvergadering, Thursday 22 October, 20:00". The skip shows as "(a message this version can't display)", as any unknown message does today.

LHow it reads in the chat

  • The plan's card names the rule and the next date, and answers on the card apply to that next date.
  • A skip or a stop is a small line in the chat, said once, rather than a second card.
  • The web page's existing "download as a calendar file" button includes the rule, so a repeating meeting downloads as a repeating event.

3What changes in the message

Plans are ActivityStreams objects sealed inside the chat (cartopolis_core::meeting, mirrored in the web's meeting.ts). Three fields are added and one kind of message. No server change and no wire protocol change, since the server can't read any of it.
