cartopolis workspace — calls that happen somewhere

Calls are the app's encrypted calls (Opus, VP9, sealed with the chat's keys, forwarded by the simulator, which cannot open them). The web UI adds nothing to the security, only to the shape: a call can be the usual grid of faces, or it can take place in the channel's place, and whoever knows the site can walk everyone through it.

DShare my view, not my screen new

  • Screen sharing a 3D city sends a blurry video of somebody else's monitor. Share my view sends only where the camera is and what it is aimed at, a few bytes per frame, and every viewer draws the city itself: full resolution, smooth on a weak connection, no lag on someone else's encoder.
  • Look around myself lets anyone leave the tour to look at something on their own. The banner then says Back to Sanne.
  • Pointing: the presenter's cursor shows up as a ring in everyone's view, so "this corner here" means the same corner for everyone.
  • Points dropped during the tour are posted to the channel, so the call leaves a record in the channel: the route walked and the spots discussed.
  • Here it is a guest workspace: the municipality's channel, with an alderman in it who never installed anything. A link opens the browser, the browser opens the place.
  • The camera position travels inside the call's encryption, like the audio does.
