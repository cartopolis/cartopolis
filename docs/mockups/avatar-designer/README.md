# Avatar designer: mockup

A design proposal, not code. Everything on it is **new**.

The MakeHuman avatar (`docs/notes/makehuman-avatars.md`) makes a person from
sliders, so choosing a look becomes shaping one rather than picking a style per
slot. v1 opens from **You › Avatar** (home-and-profile v1) as a live view of your
own avatar over a sheet of controls in four tabs: **Body** (MakeHuman's macro
sliders in plain words, body shape continuous rather than a choice of two),
**Face** (about 25 of MakeHuman's ~100 face controls, grouped by part),
**Skin & hair** (a tone over one skin texture, MakeHuman hair tinted), and
**Clothes** (one garment per slot, re-fitted to any body, tinted). The previews
are real renders of the trial avatar; the other bodies and the face sliders are
drawn, not built.

## v1
