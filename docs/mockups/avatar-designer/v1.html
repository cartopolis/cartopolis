Avatar designer v1

Opened from You › Avatar (home-and-profile v1). The top of the screen is your own avatar, drawn live by the client and turned with a finger. The sheet below holds the controls, in four tabs. Every slider is a MakeHuman control, so the body is the one MakeHuman would make from the same values, and anything worn re-fits to it. The whole look travels as a few dozen numbers, and everyone's phone builds the same person from them. Everything on this sheet is new; the slot-by-slot chooser in World's ☰ ▸ Appearance goes.

ABody

  • MakeHuman's macro sliders, renamed into plain words. Each one blends the targets crates/human already blends (macros.rs).
  • Body shape is a slider, not a choice of two. MakeHuman's gender is continuous, so anyone can sit anywhere on it. The label under the value is a word, never a number.
  • The ticks mark the average. Height shows metres, measured from the body as built.
  • Chest only does something toward the feminine end (MakeHuman has no masculine counterpart), so it dims rather than hides.
  • Undo/redo go back through every drag; the dice make a random plausible person.

BFace

  • The camera moves in to the face when this tab opens (the Body | Face switch on the left does it by hand).
  • MakeHuman has about 100 face controls (head, eyes, nose, mouth, ears, chin, cheeks). This proposes about 25, five or six per part. The chips pick the part, so the sheet never scrolls far.
  • These targets are not in the pack yet. Each body needs only the few it uses, so they could be fetched on demand (question 2).

CSkin & hair

  • Skin tone is a colour over one detailed skin texture, not one texture per tone. That keeps the download to a few textures, and any tone is possible (the swatches are presets; a long press opens a fine picker).
  • Hair styles are MakeHuman's hair, fitted to the head like clothes. The colour tints the strands. "More" opens the full list.
  • Eyebrows and lashes follow the hair colour unless changed (under "More").

DClothes

  • One item per slot: a top, bottoms, shoes, and any extras (glasses, a cap). A dress fills top and bottoms.
  • Garments are MakeHuman clothes, fitted to whatever body the sliders make (fit.rs), so no garment needs a size. Skin under a garment is removed so it cannot poke through.
  • The colour tints the garment's own texture, so the folds and seams stay.
  • While this tab is open the preview walks on the spot, because how clothes fit shows best in motion.

How it works, and what is open

  • What is saved: about forty numbers (the sliders, a skin tone, a hair style and colour, and up to six garments with a colour each). They are stored with the account, sent once to whoever can see you, and every phone builds the same person from them, in about 10 ms (measured on a Mac, 2026-10-17).
  • Others see a change when you tap Done, not while you drag.
  • Question 1, the catalogue: MakeHuman's CC0 system pack has 10 hair styles, 20 garments and 23 skins. Its community library has hundreds more, under mixed licences and of uneven quality. What ships is a choice of taste and of licence, best made from renders of each.
  • Question 2, the download: every body and face target together is about 80 MB raw, too much for the app. The proposal: ship the body sliders in a compact encoding (its size still to be measured), and fetch face targets and garments on demand, each once, then keep them.
  • Question 3, children's bodies: MakeHuman goes down to babies. This sheet starts the age slider at 18, because the world is for adults (an open question for communities that are not).
