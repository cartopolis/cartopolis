Lenses — a lens stays on, its sheet goes away v3

Asked 2026-11-02, against v2: "why can't a lens be on, and its dialog be clicked away? The lens icon can say whether a lens is on." So on and shown are two things. A lens on is the coloured map, the frame round the screen and the lens disc lit; nothing else needs to stand over the map. The sheet is where you read and choose, and it goes when you are done: ✕, a pick, or a drag on the map hides it. Turning the lens off is its own button. The same on every screen.

3bThe sheet — read and choose

  • One sheet holds it all: the open figure's key (scale, year, the place under the crosshair) over the figures to pick from.

3cOff

  • No lens: the disc is dark and opens the shelf of lenses. Picking one there turns it on and hides the shelf — 3a.
