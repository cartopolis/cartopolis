UX review · what gets in the way, and what to draw instead

Requested 2026-11-04: "analyse cartopolis ui/ux and suggest improvements, make mockups first". The 1 Nov round (ui-directions/) was about how it looks: accents, button weights, three skeletons. This one is about what a person runs into: the first launch, finding people, going somewhere, the community page, leaving safely. The "Today" screens were captured from main on 2026-11-04 with docs/phone-ui-shots.toml (412×900 at scale 1.5, mac-1). Every "After" is new, drawn in today's teal glass and today's dock, so each fix can land without direction C. C's tab bar would also solve problem 1.

1The social half is invisible

Signed out, nothing on the map says that people, chats or communities exist. Signing in takes 2 taps, then you have to type a server name, and the app has no way to make an account.

2Buttons say how it works, not what you'll get

From / To / Beam, "to Route end", "Needs a GPS position", "Offline — the map only", "simulator", "Under", "Polaroid". Each one makes you translate.

3Chores push the content down

A community opens on its moderation queue, and a building card opens on nine numbers. What people come for is further down or behind a tab.

4Leaving is risky, and Back is unpredictable

Log out, Block and Delete act at once and look like ordinary buttons. On Android, Back with a card open sends the whole app away, while on a page stack it drops every page at once.

1First launch: show that there are people, then ask

  • Today you get a silent offline map. Signing in is Menu ▸ Sign in…, and its only explanation is a hover tooltip, which a phone never shows (ui.rs:177).
  • One card, once, over the map: what is around here (counts only, no names), Sign in, Create account, and "just the map". It stays closed after the first "not now" and comes back as the People disc.
  • The dock gets labels and a People disc that opens Community. Signed out, that disc opens this card. The Camera moves into More (it is already missing on the web), so the dock still has five places.
  • The left column becomes one capsule (layers, lens, compass). The clock moves into Layers, since it changes the light, not the view.

2Sign in: nothing to know beforehand, and a way in for newcomers

  • Today the Server field starts empty (login_screen.rs:131), with only a hint. The Today shot shows it filled in only because the capture seeds it (seed_for_capture). You have to know a server name before anything happens. The default becomes cartopolis.org, and choosing another server is a link.
  • Create an account: the website already has a sign-up (atlas/web/src/lib/signup.ts). The app opens it, or shows the same form.
  • The provider buttons are shown at once, but only for the providers the server actually offers. None is set up yet (see the external sign-in memory), so today this list would be empty.
  • Errors are in plain words, on the card: never "No simulator is listening there" or a raw OS error (net/network.rs:410).

3A place: one obvious next step

  • Today there are three buttons of equal weight: From · To · Save, and "Beam" for teleporting. "From" comes first, but the thing you most often want is to get there.
  • The main button names what will happen and how long it takes: Walk here · 6 min (walking first, as in the routing direction). Save is the bookmark icon. Everything else goes in ⋯, in words: "Start a route here" and "Jump there" instead of From and Beam.
  • The ring (long press) uses the same words. Today it says "Route here / Route from / Go there" (ui.rs:831) while the card says To / From / Beam.

4A route: name both ends, and explain the greyed-out option

  • Today it says "1.8 km · to Route end", picks car first, and has a footnote, "Needs a GPS position", that does not say what needs it.
  • The modes are listed walk, bike, car. Car is greyed out, and the line under it says why and how to turn it on (a tap, not a hover).
  • Both ends have names, so you can read the route without looking at the map. The first action is to go: "Start walking", with "End route" beside it.

6The menu: who you are, at the top; developer tools out of sight

  • Today the menu is Sign in / Log out first (with no confirmation, styled like Exit), then Settings and About, then Developer tools and Share diagnostics for everyone, then Exit. Who you're signed in as appears only in a tooltip (ui.rs:169).
  • The top row is you, written out, and it opens the You page. Sign out is last, in red, and asks first.
  • Developer tools and diagnostics move to Settings ▸ Advanced, off by default. Ctrl+Shift+D still works. Exit stays on desktop only, since Android apps don't quit.
  • One name for one thing: "Sign out" everywhere (today it is also "Log out"), "Avatar" everywhere (today it is also "Appearance"), and "Photo from the world" instead of "Polaroid".

7Leaving safely: say what happens, and let it be undone

  • Today Block is a plain button beside "Message privately", and it acts at once (chat.rs:826). The same goes for the ring's Delete and for Log out. Bookmarks, contacts and admin already arm first and confirm on a second tap. Destructive actions get one pattern, in red, saying what will happen.
  • Anything that can be undone shows a short Undo note at the top instead of asking first: leaving a community, removing a saved place. There is no undo note anywhere today.
  • Back closes the innermost thing, one step at a time, including cards. This is what ui-vocabulary.md already promises.
