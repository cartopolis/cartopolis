Walking, the camera, and posts

Out walking with the GPS, the view should be yours: from where you stand, looking where the phone looks. The camera gets its own button in the dock and three modes — In world, Mixed, Real — and every picture remembers exactly where it was taken. Posting is one tap from the picture or from a long press on the ground, and a community's posts can be shown on their own, with its chat out of the way until you want it.

Proposal v3, 2026-10-18: walking with the GPS is seen from where you stand; every button is round and the same size; a public post keeps the full pose. Grey controls are the app as it is today; anything marked new does not exist yet. The world pictures are Cartopolis captures and the photo a stand-in (credits at the foot).

1Walking with the GPS: the view from where you stand

Today the GPS only moves the avatar; the Follow camera is the ordinary chase camera, which sits 4.5 m above the feet and 5.2 m behind, looking 26° down, and keeps whatever tilt and zoom you last left it at. The GPS height never reaches the camera, so it is these numbers, not the receiver.

2One camera, three modes new

The orange dock button opens the camera in the mode you used last. Swipe or tap the mode list beside the shutter; the round button under it flips to a selfie in every mode. The camera turns with the phone: landscape and portrait both work, in every mode, and the picture comes out the way you held it.

5What a picture remembers, and what goes

Every picture from the camera (all three modes) carries its own pose in the file's EXIF, enough for the agent or the shot harness to put the camera back on the same view. That is what the field check was for, so the field check goes.

Developer tools ▸ View ▸ Field check, its capture screen, the three-panel picture (real, Cartopolis, aerial) and the pose text under it. Mixed mode makes the two-panel picture; the aerial, if the agent wants it, is rendered from the EXIF.

Kept: reading the GPS, compass and receiver, and the harness's roll, fov and date keys, which a pose from EXIF still needs.

Pictures keep their EXIF

Today every picture sent in a chat is re-encoded and loses its EXIF. A camera picture keeps its block through that, so sending it to the agent is enough.

Public posts too

A posted picture keeps the full pose like any other (decided 2026-10-18). It is public and federates, so anyone who saves it can read where you stood, to the centimetre with RTK, and when; the post already shows the place.
