Cartopolis chat — a messenger with a world attached

Mockup, not code. The app opens as a messenger. Every conversation has a second view: the 3D world, showing where the people in that conversation are. One toggle switches between the two, and both views share the same messages and the same text field.

1 · Every chat has a place

A direct message shows where the other person is. A group shows its members on the map. Here is the chat of whoever is standing near you: it's the existing Local channel.

2 · One thread, two views

Chat | World doesn't change the conversation. It changes what fills the screen behind it. The text field stays put, so you can keep typing while you switch.

3 · Orange means "in the world"

The brand orange is used for one thing only: live presence. An orange ring, dot or button always means that person is on the map right now and you can go to them.

4 · Sharing where you are is opt-in, per chat, and time-limited

Nobody appears on anyone's map by default. You share your position with one chat, for a set time, and you can stop at any moment. The existing block and report tools apply unchanged.

4The toggle is also a drag handle

  • Dragging the sheet between Chat and World gives a half-and-half view: map on top, messages underneath. The toggle jumps between the two ends and the handle covers everything in between.
  • In a group, the map shows members who have chosen to share their location, plus whatever the chat is about: here, a shared route drawn on the real streets.
  • Tap a route or place card and the map above scrolls to it. The chat stays open.

Wide screens: no toggle, both views at once

On a desktop or tablet the chat list and the open chat sit on either side of the world. Choosing a chat moves the camera to its members, like picking a chat on a phone and switching to World.

What already exists and what would be new

PieceNotes
Local, Global and named room channelsexistsThese become the Here, Global and #room rows in the chat list.
Speech bubbles over avatars, fading transcriptexistsThis is the World tab's way of showing new messages. It already runs without a panel.
Place links (/go?place=), routing, proximity voiceexistsThese become the place card, the "On my way" / "Walk to" card, and the Talk button.
Block, report, delete accountexistsThey stay on the server and work the same in every chat.
Direct messagesnewThe server only knows about Local, Global and Room chats today. DMs would need a new message type (a protocol change).
Stored history and unread countsnewLocal is never stored, and a chat list needs history plus a "last read" marker kept on the server.
Per-chat, time-limited location sharingnewThis is the core of the concept, and the main privacy decision. Today avatars are only visible to people nearby in the same area.
World view scoped to one chatnewOnly this chat's members get a name and bubble, and the camera moves to show them all. Everyone else stays a dot.
