Cartopolis chat — v5: the essence

A chat is a conversation about places. Chat shows what was said; World shows the same chat on the map: the people in it and the places they pointed at. One new kind of message makes it work: a point, a spot in the world seen from where the sender stood.

1Every chat has two views

Chat | World in the header. Same messages, same text field. World isn't a separate map feature: it is the chat, laid out on the map.

2You can point at the world

Tap anything on the map and send it to a chat. It arrives as a picture of what you saw. Tap it and you're standing there. On the World view it's a numbered pin.

3People show up where they are

An avatar if they're in the world. Their real location only if they chose to share it with this chat, until a time they chose.

CPointing at something

  • Today's building card, unchanged, plus one row: your recent chats. One tap sends the point.
  • The same row goes on every card: a place, a search result, a route, a spot you long-pressed.

Stays exactly as today

  • The map viewer (the World tab outside a chat), camera modes, layers, search, places.
  • Routing and driver mode, reached with Go from any point or card.
  • Nearby chat, speech bubbles, voice, emotes, block and report. Nearby chat becomes the "Here" chat in the list.
  • Signed out: the viewer as it is now; Chats only says it needs an account.

New, and nothing else

  • Point: one new message type (a place, a camera position, a picture).
  • Chat | World on every chat.
  • Direct messages and stored history, which a chat list needs anyway.
  • Sharing real location with one chat, until a time.
  • Left for later, and only if the core works: looking together through a friend's camera, meet-ups, the ring of actions around avatars.
