Cartopolis chat — v3: real location, getting around, routes

Follows up v1 and v2. Four changes: your real location (from your phone's GPS) is now clearly separate from your avatar; the World tab is today's app with its camera modes; routing and driving work exactly as they do now, with or without chat; and people and places from your chats become extra destinations.

Two kinds of "where"

Orange is your avatar: where you are in the 3D world. Blue is your real location: where your phone is. They never share a colour, a shape or a button.

Blue is always visible

While anyone can see your real location, a blue bar at the top of every screen says who and for how long. Tap it to stop. It's never hidden, even while driving.

Pick people, not a setting

Sharing always starts from a list of names with none ticked except the chat you're in. A group shows its size and needs one extra confirmation.

The World tab is today's app

Search, Places, Route, Layers, and the Follow · Fly · Earth camera all stay. Chats only add people to the map, and you don't need an avatar to look around.
