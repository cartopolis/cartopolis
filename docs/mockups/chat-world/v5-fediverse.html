Cartopolis chat — v5 addendum: the fediverse

Yes. With ActivityPub, Cartopolis becomes a fediverse server of its own, the way Pixelfed is one for photos: every account is also @name@cartopolis.org, which anyone on Mastodon can find, follow and reply to. Cartopolis's part in the fediverse is places: it publishes points and shows other people's located posts on the map. It doesn't try to be a general Mastodon app. Private chats stay encrypted and never cross over.

Goes out to the fediverse

  • Points you post publicly, with picture, place and a map link
  • Your public profile: name, picture, bio, links
  • Your replies to fediverse posts
  • Deletes when you delete a post or your account

Comes in from the fediverse

  • Follows of your account (you can choose to approve them)
  • Replies, boosts and likes on your points
  • Posts with a place from people you follow, onto the map
  • Reports from other servers about your users, and yours to them

Never crosses over

  • Private chats and everything in them: points, pictures, shared real locations
  • Avatars, voice, nearby chat: real-time, and run by the simulator
  • Your real location, in any form
  • Who you chat with inside Cartopolis

What it takes on the server

  • An address for every account: WebFinger at cartopolis.org/.well-known/webfinger, so @jeroen@cartopolis.org resolves. Caddy routes it to the simulator, like /api today.
  • An "actor" per account: a public profile document with an inbox and an outbox. The server signs everything it sends with a per-account server key, which is separate from the encryption keys and never touches private chats.
  • An outgoing queue with retries: posting to 200 followers on 60 servers is 60 deliveries, some of which fail and must be retried. It runs beside the simulator's tick loop, never on it.
  • An incoming inbox that checks each delivery's signature, then stores replies, follows and located posts.
  • A picture proxy: phones fetch fediverse pictures through Cartopolis, so a remote server never learns a Cartopolis user's IP address from a map pin.
  • Server-level moderation: block whole servers, filter incoming posts with the same word filter, send and receive reports (ActivityPub's Flag).
  • A library, not our own implementation: activitypub_federation (from the Lemmy project) handles signatures, WebFinger and fetching, and has an axum integration; the simulator's HTTP side is already axum. To be checked before relying on it.
  • Order: the fediverse proof (C) needs none of this and can ship with encrypted chat. Following and posting come later, as their own step.
