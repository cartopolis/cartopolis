Cartopolis chat — v5 addendum: contacts and message requests

Today anybody can open a private chat with anybody by typing a username. This adds two things: contacts (people you have accepted, so they can message you directly) and message requests (a stranger's first message waits in a separate tray until you accept it). Most people meet in the world, so that is where you add someone. A link or a username is the other way in.

You meet people in the world

Tap an avatar to see who it is and send a first message. The request says where you met (“both at Vismarkt, 5 min ago”), which is the context a stranger's message usually lacks.

A first message is a request

It lands under Requests, not in your chats. Until you accept, a stranger can send text only: points, pictures and shared locations are held back. They can't tell whether you have read or declined it.

Contacts are mutual

Accepting makes you contacts both ways. A contact messages you straight into your chats, and can see that you're online if you allow it. Removing a contact doesn't block them. Block and report are separate.

The server enforces it

Who may send you a request, and how many a new account can send, are checked on the server, like blocks already are. The chat stays end-to-end encrypted, including the first message.

How it fits what exists today

  • Today: typing a username in New chat starts an encrypted chat at once. Blocks, reports and the word filter exist. There are no contacts.
  • A request is an encrypted chat the recipient hasn't joined yet. Their device holds the invitation. Accept joins it, decline leaves it. The server sees that a request exists (it already sees who is in which chat), never what it says.
  • The server checks the recipient's “who can reach me” setting, the one-message limit and the per-day limit for new accounts before it passes an invitation on.
  • “Met at Vismarkt” comes from the server, which already knows who stood near whom for the Here chat. It's shown only to the two people involved.

Left out on purpose

  • Following people: dropped on 2026-09-26. You follow places, not people.
  • Suggested friends, a people directory, importing your phone contacts: each gives away who uses Cartopolis.
  • Nicknames and contact groups: later, if the list grows long.
  • Verifying a contact in person: the key-change warning stays the only protection until proofs come back.
