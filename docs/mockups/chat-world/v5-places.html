Cartopolis chat — v5 addendum: follow a place

What ActivityPub adds is that a place can be followed. Every area on the map (a square, a neighbourhood, a town) is a public community with its own fediverse address, such as @binnenstad.groningen@cartopolis.org. Following it, from Cartopolis or from Mastodon, means hearing what's posted there. Posting there is just posting a point inside it, or mentioning it from Mastodon.

1A community is a chat with an area

It's the same shape as v5: a Chat view of its posts and a World view where they're pins inside its outline. It just happens to be public and anchored to a place.

2Places nest, so posts rise

A point posted at the Vismarkt belongs to Vismarkt, Binnenstad and Groningen at once. Follow as close or as wide as you like; you hear it once.

3Anyone on the fediverse can join in

From Mastodon you follow @binnenstad.groningen@cartopolis.org like any account. Mention it in a post and the post lands on the map there, even though Mastodon itself has no location feature.

DThe same place, from Mastodon later

  • A place is a fediverse group account, as Lemmy communities and Guppe groups already are. Mastodon users follow it and see its posts as boosts.
  • Mentioning the place is how someone outside posts there. No Cartopolis account needed.
  • Nothing about a follower's own location is involved. They choose a place by name.

Where the places come from

  • Areas from boundary data: municipality, district and neighbourhood (in the Netherlands, the CBS districts and neighbourhoods; elsewhere OpenStreetMap's administrative boundaries). The building card already shows a building's district.
  • Named spots from the map: squares, parks, stations, streets; the same places the map already labels.
  • Communities that aren't an official area (#fietsers-groningen) can be created with an outline drawn on the map. They behave the same.
  • A place's community exists from the start, but only really "opens" (gets an actor on the fediverse) after its first post, so there aren't a million empty accounts.

What stays the same

  • Private chats: encrypted, never on the fediverse. Points sent there stay there.
  • Here: live, nearby avatars, not kept, not federated. Places are the lasting version of "near".
  • Nobody's real location is used. You follow a place by choice; a post's position is the point you made, not where your phone is.
  • Posting is a point: the ActivityStreams object from the earlier addendum, addressed to the place instead of to a person.

Open questions

  • Who moderates a place? The operator at first. Local moderators per area later, perhaps the municipality's own account for official news.
  • Handles: @binnenstad.groningen keeps names unique (many towns have a Binnenstad). Needs one naming rule, fixed before the first place federates.
  • Seeing a post twice on Mastodon if you follow both the Vismarkt and Binnenstad: inside Cartopolis you see it once; on Mastodon it's up to the app showing the boosts.
