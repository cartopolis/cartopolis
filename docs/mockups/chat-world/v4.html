Cartopolis chat — v4: people and the world, one gesture

v3 put a messenger next to the map. v4 joins them: anything you can tap in the world can go into a chat, and anything in a chat opens back into the world, at the exact spot and camera angle it was sent from. Items marked today exist in the app now; new marks everything this design adds.

1 · Tap anything

A building, a shop, a bench, a person, an empty street: tap it and get one card with the same four actions every time: Share · Go · Save and one that depends on the thing (Look inside, Sit, Wave).

2 · Share what you see, not a link

Sharing sends the thing and your view of it: a picture plus the exact camera position. The person who receives it taps Look from here and stands where you stood, facing where you faced. Place links already carry that camera position.

3 · Three ways to be together

Look together: follow a friend's camera and see what they point at, with no avatars needed. In the world: avatars side by side that can wave, sit and talk. For real: meet up, using your real location.

4 · Real location needs a reason

The usual way to share it is a meet-up: it starts shortly before the agreed time and stops by itself when you arrive. Sharing by hand still exists, with a list of names and an end time.

6Meet up: real location with a start and an end new

  • A meet-up is a place, a time and who's coming. Saying "I'm going" asks once: share my real location with the others going, from 15 minutes before until I arrive.
  • On the day the card fills in by itself: who's there, who's 3 minutes away. Pieter didn't agree, so he shows as "not sharing" and nothing more.
  • Sharing stops on arrival or when the meet-up is over. You never have to remember to switch it off.

  • Today's driver mode today: turn banner, GPS arrow, stripped-down map, camera looking ahead.
