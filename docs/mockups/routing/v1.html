Routing — v1: walk in the world, ride and drive with a GPS, meet through a chat

Every frame is a real capture of today's phone build (412×915, the shot harness, Groningen) with the proposal drawn over it. Three journeys: Walk happens in the world — the avatar, the map and the follow camera stay. Bike and Car are real-world navigation and use today's driver mode, which needs a position fix. And a friend's chat is where a route starts when the point of it is to meet somebody.

3Meeting a friend: v4's meet-up, with a route for each person

The meet-up is the one already designed in chat-world v4 (screen 6): a place, a time and who's coming, where saying I'm going asks once to share your real location with the others going, from 15 minutes before until you arrive. This adds three things: Route on everything in a chat, a suggested place that is fair to both of you, and each person's route on the chat's map. All of it travels inside the chat's end-to-end encrypted messages.
