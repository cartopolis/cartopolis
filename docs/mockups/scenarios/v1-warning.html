Later: early warning for citizens new

The same region states, on a phone, for one person. Cartopolis is not an alarm authority and must never act like one: in Live it shows what NL-Alert (or another country's official service) sent, puts it on the map with the threat's direction and time, and connects people to each other — "I'm safe", who in my street needs help. In Exercise it lets a household or a municipality practise the same screens, unmistakably striped.

Official, or striped

A red alert card appears only for a message from an official feed, quoted as sent. Everything else wears stripes.

The map adds why and when

Your region's state, the direction it comes from, how long you have — the parts a text message cannot draw.

People, not just pins

Check in once; the people and communities you chose see it. Neighbours can ask for and offer help.

Works badly connected

The message and the advice show without the map; the map follows when the network allows.

Rules for Live

  • Only an official feed opens a red card; its text is shown as sent, with the sender.
  • Cartopolis's own reading (times, direction) is labelled as such, and is left out when the feed gives no area or time.
  • No model or exercise ever colours a region in Live, and no alarm sound outside Live.
  • The all-clear is the feed's, not ours.

What it would need

  • Access to NL-Alert's messages in machine-readable form (it is a cell broadcast today, not a public API) — or another official source that is.
  • Safety regions and municipalities as region shapes.
  • Check-ins on the existing encrypted chat, so the server never learns who is where.
  • Push notifications on Android, and a text-only path when the map cannot load.
