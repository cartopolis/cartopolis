The macro map, taken further towards Civilization new

The painted far map landed on 2026-10-31 (macro-view v4). Civilization's strategic view works because every part of the screen answers a question at a glance: whose land is this, what is it worth, what changed since I last looked, what don't I know yet. This sheet borrows those answers for a map of communities — and leaves out what makes it a game: no score, no winning, no opponents.

A board, not a picture

Hexes are the unit everything aggregates to: a lens, a community's reach, a scenario's field. Hover one and it says what it holds.

What changed, in one column

Like the turn notifications: a short stack on the right of what happened since you last looked; each one flies you there.

Honest about gaps

Fog of war becomes fog of data: where the map knows nothing recent it says so, instead of looking as sure as everywhere else.

One clock

The same time bar serves live (now), history (last week) and a scenario (T+3:20). Scenarios are the next sheets.
