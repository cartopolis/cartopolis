# Layers by where they are, and a ring of actions

## v1 — proposed 2026-10-08

![v1](v1.png)

Two proposals in one sheet. The Layers panel's ~40 checkboxes regrouped into
six categories by *where* the thing is (Map, City, Places, Moving, Under,
Orbit), each layer a tile that is its own switch. And the text context menu
replaced by the ring of round buttons chat-world v4 drew for people, used for
every kind of thing you can tap: a point on the map, an object, a satellite.
Screen 8 is the camera attachment ("Following") built alongside it.

Agreed 2026-10-08 ("looks good, build") and built the same day, with the
recommended answers to the open questions: the Life master switch removed, the
ring replacing the map's context menu everywhere, and street names, road
markings and moving water kept under Map. Built differently from the drawing:
the sheet is the existing right-hand panel (a full-width panel on a phone)
rather than a half-height bottom sheet, and Flights and Ships are not shown as
placeholder tiles until they exist.
