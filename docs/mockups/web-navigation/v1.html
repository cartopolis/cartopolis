cartopolis on the web: Community | World, and Admin beside them

The web page gets the app's switch, worded Community | World. World is the real web build of the app (the WebAssembly viewer at /viewer/), running inside the page rather than linked to. For an operator a third segment, Admin, puts the admin pages in the same window instead of a separate site. Today the page is one flat list with links at the bottom; this gives it four levels you can always see and step back through.

Level 1 · top bar

Community | World | Admin

The switch, next to the wordmark in a top bar that is always there. Admin shows only to accounts that can manage something. The switch keeps your place: World opens on the community you were reading.

Level 2 · rail

Where

A rail of round tiles: Home (people and groups) and one tile per community, plus Find and Start. In Admin the rail becomes the admin areas.

CommunityHome · Oosterpoort · FietsclubAdminPeople · Content · Data
Level 3 · sidebar

What, inside it

The community's topics, and for a moderator its requests and reports. For an admin area, its pages (Accounts, Roles, Claims…).

Level 4 · side pane

Detail

A thread, a person, a community's info, or the record an admin page is editing. Closes with ✕; never replaces what is behind it.

BWorld

  • Signed out, World still works: it is the map, with no rail and no chat.

CAdmin

  • A third segment, in gold new, so it never reads as part of the game. Only an account with at least one admin page sees it; the others see Community | World. /admin keeps working as an address.
  • The seven tabs grouped into three areas on the rail: People (Accounts, Roles, Building claims, Reports), Content (Overrides, Models) and Data (Pipeline, Autopilot). Today they are one list of seven.
  • An account opens beside the list, with tabs for its roles, its buildings and what it did, rather than as a form that replaces the list. The side pane already exists in the admin page; this gives it a header and tabs.
  • Message goes straight to Community with a chat to that person open, and a building on an account opens in World. The three halves link into each other instead of being separate sites.
  • The admin pages today sign in separately, with their own session. Here they share the chat's sign-in; the server still checks every admin call.
