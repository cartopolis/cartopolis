Cartopolis — field check v3: what the position knows

The receiver knows far more than where you are, and every bit of it is a check on the world. Its height checks our ground against the national height map. Lining up the ghost measures the compass, check after check. And touching something with the receiver, a building corner, a kerb, a pole, checks the registers to the centimetre, with no photo at all.

Orange values are real (the BAG outline, the AHN height, the correction station and its distance). Italic values are example readings from a receiver that was not there.

1Height: our ground vs measured ground

Receiver height minus the antenna's height above ground, converted to NAP, against AHN and against the ground Cartopolis draws from it. Measured vs AHN tests the source; AHN vs drawn tests our grading.

2Heading: the compass, measured

Lining up the ghost gives the true heading; the compass gave another. Both are kept. Over a walk that is the compass's offset, and the places where it goes wild (steel, bikes, cars).

3Touch: the registers, to the centimetre

Hold the receiver against a building corner, a kerb or a pole and tap. The point is compared with where the BAG and BGT put that object, and with where Cartopolis draws it.

  • Each check stands on its own; a walk of them gives the patterns: a ground fault in one place, a compass offset everywhere.
  • Only differences bigger than the errors on both sides become fixes.

How much to trust a check

The quality rows decide it, not a feeling. Float instead of fixed: position only, no centimetre claims. Corrections more than ~10 s old: same. Station distance adds about 1 cm per 10 km to the error. No antenna height: no height check.

Already in the app

The receiver reports its error per direction, satellites, HDOP, the age of the corrections and their station; the nearest open station is chosen from Kadaster's table. Atlas serves the AHN ground height. The client has the BAG outlines.

New

A NAP height from the receiver: its altitude needs the Dutch geoid model (NLGEO2018, the RDNAPTRANS conversion), not the rough geoid the receiver reports. The antenna height setting. The touch check screen. Keeping both headings per check. The fuller strip.

Open

A touch on something the registers do not have (a pole on the Grote Markt) is a measurement, not a check. Whether those may become world content is the user's call: the rule so far is real registers only.
