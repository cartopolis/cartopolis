Cartopolis — field check v1: one tap, two pictures

On a walk with the RTK receiver, one tap in a chat takes two pictures from the same spot: the phone's camera, and Cartopolis drawn from exactly that position, heading and lens. They arrive in the chat as one message, so whoever is on the other end, a person or the agent, can say what the world gets wrong there.

1The shutter takes both

No separate screenshot step. The pose is read at the instant of the photo, and the render is made from that pose, so the two can't drift apart while you walk.

2The render follows the lens

Rear camera: Cartopolis looks where the phone points. Front camera: it turns round, and looks at what is behind you. Flip switches both together.

3The pose travels with it

Position and its accuracy, heading, tilt and lens go in the message. The agent can re-render the same view itself, with layers off or the aerial beside it, instead of trusting one picture.

  • The agent doesn't need the render to be right: from the pose it can shoot the same view itself (--shot --at … --look …), with layers off, or with --aerial-reference beside it.

Already in the app

  • The RTK receiver as a position source, with its accuracy.
  • Both cameras through Camera2: the front one for calls, the back one (greyscale) for precise tracking, with the lens read from the phone.
  • The phone's attitude (heading, tilt) from the orientation sensors.
  • Pictures in chats, and an agent account that can read and answer a chat.
  • Rendering a given pose off-screen, in the shot harness, desktop and headless only.

New

  • A colour still from either camera, taken in the app so the pose is read at the shutter.
  • A second render on the phone from the camera's pose and lens, alongside the map.
  • The capture screen: live corner render, ghost, flip.
  • The paired message and its Compare view.
  • Weakest link: the compass. 3° of heading is 6 m sideways at the tower's 120 m. Open question: may the ghost be dragged to line up, with the correction stored beside the measured heading?
