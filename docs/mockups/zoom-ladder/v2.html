The zoom ladder v2: a slimmer rail, the light as a theme switch

After review (2026-11-01): the light switch belongs with the other light settings — Auto, Light, Dark, like a theme switcher — and the rail was too heavy for what it does. Everything else on v1 stands (the grab-and-throw globe, flights, the three rungs above the Earth). Orange marks what is new.

1The globe, with the slim rail and the light disc

Desktop, 8,000 km over Europe, light set to Light. The rail is a 4 px track between a small − (out, towards the Milky Way) and + (in, towards the street), 30 px wide in all; only the rung you are on is named. The light disc joins the column top left, under locate.

2The light: Auto, Light, Dark

These are today's sun modes (Settings ▸ World: Automatic, Day, Night, Manual) given a theme switcher's names and a disc on the map. Nothing new to learn: one setting, the same at every height, with what it means from above made useful.
