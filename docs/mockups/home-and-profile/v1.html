Community tab, profile and settings v1: less on the list, a home for you

A design proposal, not code. The list keeps only conversations; making something new moves to a sheet; the people button becomes your own disc, which opens a profile page, and from there Settings, now including access tokens (today only on the web page). The tab is renamed to match the web page's Community | World. new marks what the app does not have today.

0Today

  • "Chats" is said twice (the title and the switch), and the list holds more than chats.
  • The new-chat choices open inside the list and push it down, with a two-line encryption note.
  • The person button at the top is Contacts, but looks like "me". There is no profile, and Settings is only in World's ☰ menu.
  • Every community and every topic is its own row; "Voice and blocked people" sits at the bottom of the list.

AThe list

  • No title: the switch says where you are. Its first half is renamed Community, as on the web page.
  • Here and Everyone, the two public chats, share one row as tiles.
  • Then one list, newest first: people, groups and communities together. A community is one row; its preview names the topic that last spoke, and a tap opens the community (or its only topic).
  • The padlock stays on private rows. Voice and blocked people move to Settings.

DSettings as a list

  • Today's five tabs (General, World, Graphics, Audio, Account) become pages in a list, which fits a phone better than five tabs in a row. Their contents do not change.
  • Two pages join: Privacy and safety (the block list, out of the chat list, and the request policy) and Access tokens. The voice switch that sat beside the blocks goes to Sound and voice.
  • The same screen opens from World's ☰ menu, as Settings does today.
