A white room to design an object in

The Construct in The Matrix: an endless white space with nothing in it but the thing you are working on. Here it is a separate mode, entered with Design here from the same ring that holds Build. The map fades out, you are left with the object, your avatar for scale, a metre grid and a faint outline of the spot it is meant for. When it is done, Place fades the world back in with the object standing where you tapped. Everything marked new does not exist today.

Nothing but the object

No buildings to orbit around, no traffic, no weather, no other people walking through your work. Plain studio light, so a colour is the colour you picked and not the colour of dusk.

It remembers where it goes

The room is opened for a spot. North points the way it does there, and the kerb and facade lines of the spot are drawn faintly on the floor, so a stall that is too deep for the pavement shows it here.

The same tools, more room

The Edit dialog's Basics, Shape and Surface tabs, docked on the right instead of a floating window, plus a parts list on the left. Learning the room is learning Build.

Nobody sees it until it is placed

A design is a draft on your device. Others see nothing while you work, not a half-made box flickering on the square. Placing sends it to the server as one act.

2Design here, beside Build

  • Design here new sits next to Build today: Build drops a box on the spot and edits it in place, Design takes you away to make it first.
  • On an object you may change, the object's ring gets Design too: it opens that object in the room.
  • Unlike Build it also works offline: a draft needs no server. Only Place does.

3The world fades to white

  • About half a second: the white spreads out from the tapped point while the camera sinks to eye level beside it, so it is clear where you went from.
  • The map stops streaming while you are in the room (nothing new is fetched or built); chat and calls stay connected and show as a badge.
  • Back to the map runs the same fade the other way and returns the camera to where it was.

4The room on a phone

  • One finger turns around the object, two fingers pan and pinch zooms, as on the map. Tap a part to select it.
  • The bottom sheet is the same panel as on the desktop: parts as chips, then the selected part's tabs. Drag it up for the full dialog.
  • The lamp button switches Studio light to the spot's real sun and time, for a last look before placing.

5Back on the map, placed

  • Place fades the world back in with the stall already standing on the spot, sent to the server as one act. Others see it appear whole.
  • Undo on the chip takes it back off the map and back into your drafts, for a few seconds.
  • Adjusting it in place afterwards is today's Edit; going back into the room is Design on its ring.

Open questions, with a recommendation each

QuestionRecommendedWhy, and what it costs
One object of several parts, or several objects?Several parts, placed as one group newToday an object is one shape, and a stall is seven. The first version can place seven objects that remember they were made together; a real linked object (move one, move all) is a wire change and comes second.
Where does a draft live?On the device, in Data storageNo server change and works offline. The price: a draft does not follow you to another device. Keep as draft lists them under Places ▸ Drafts.
Can I walk around in the room?No, the avatar stands as a scale figureWalking needs the follow camera and the sim; neither belongs in a private room. A later toggle could let you step in to check a doorway or a seat height.
Show the spot's outline by default?Yes, faintIt is what makes the room a room for a place rather than an empty editor. Off for anyone designing something that will travel (a model for the library).
Studio light or the spot's own?Studio by default, the spot's sun one tap awayStudio light is honest about colour; the real sun at 14:20 shows how it will actually look. Both are already in the app: the room just pins the time.
Can it become a model in the shared library?Not in v1That means exporting shapes to a glTF the simulator then validates. Worth doing, but it is its own piece of work.
Does Build stay?YesFor one box on a spot, the room is a detour. Build is the quick act, Design the considered one.
