v4: the design room as an editor, and the AI building in it

Asked on 2026-11-19, after the first room was built: a way out that is always there, a grid you can read sizes off, tools for building by hand, groups, scripts you can manage and watch, and the AI building through Midas while you watch and steer. A complete editing setup, closer to Blender than to a map. Everything marked new does not exist today; today is built.

1 · One way out, always in the same place

← Back to the map at the top left, and Esc. The street comes back through a white fade; what you built stays as a draft, not half in the world. The map's own buttons are gone while you design (today).

2 · A grid you can read sizes off

Red X (east) and blue Z (south) axes through the spot, metre lines and darker 5 m lines, a navigation gizmo that turns the view and snaps to Top / Front / Side, a 1.75 m figure, and dimensions on whatever is selected.

3 · Blender's tools, sized for a finger too

Select, move, rotate and scale (G R S), snap to the grid or to another part's face, align, duplicate, mirror, a row of copies (array), measure. Every one is a key on a desktop and a button on a phone.

4 · Groups that really exist

Ctrl-G groups, Alt-G ungroups, in the outliner or on the ring. A group moves, copies and is kept as one; inside it, each part still edits on its own. This needs a group on each object on the wire (v3's change 5).

5 · Scripts you can see working

A Scripts tab lists every script in the design, which object it sits on, and whether it is running, stopped or failing. While one runs, each call it makes lights up the object it touches and leaves a line in the log.

6 · The AI builds in front of you

From a Claude session on the Midas page: the AI opens the room on your device, builds with the same tools you have, and you watch it happen. Pause, take over, undo its last step, or point at a part and tell it what to change.

1 · The editor, on a desktop

The room keeps v3's white, now with the axes, measurements and the tools around it. Left: the tool column and the outliner (the design's tree, with its groups). Right: properties for whatever is selected. Bottom: the log of what happened, by whom (you, the AI, a script), each line undoable.

3 · The AI builds while you watch, through Midas

The person asks in a Claude session on the Midas page — on the phone, or beside the app on a desktop. The session reaches the app over the Midas device link (today: the paired phone's MCP tools, and a node's headless app), opens the room on that device, and builds with the same operations the editor has. The app shows it happening; the person steers in the session or in the room.

bThe room, while the AI builds

  • Pause holds the session's next tool call until you resume; Take over ends its turn in the room and gives you the tools; Undo its last step takes back the last AI line in the log. All three are refused to the AI, never to the person.

  • What the AI can do is exactly what the account can do: it builds as you, through the server's own checks.
