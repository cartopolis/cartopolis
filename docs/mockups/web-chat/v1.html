Web chat: a Home list you can read, and groups you can leave

The Community page's Home sidebar (sideBody in components/pages/chat/chat.tsx) and what can be done to a group once it exists. Today a group can't be left, renamed or deleted by anyone, including an admin. That is how oosterparkwijk got stuck. Drawn in the web UI's own tokens (src/css/theme.css, dark half). Marked NEW where it does not exist today.

2Leaving and deleting a group, and what an admin can do

None of this exists. It needs a protocol revision and a server change, modelled on how communities already do it (communities::leave, communities::delete).

Delete oosterparkwijk for everyone?

You made this group, so you can delete it. It disappears for mei too, and the server deletes its sealed messages.

This can't be undone.

Cancel
Delete group

Delete for everyone, offered to the group's maker (e2ee_groups.created_by). Every member's device is told and forgets the group, the way a deleted community's topics are forgotten (e2ee::topics_left, widened from topics to groups).

Leave Street party crew?

The other 3 people keep the group. You stop receiving it, and this browser forgets its keys, so you can't read it again unless somebody adds you back.

Cancel
Leave

Leave, for anyone in a group. The server stops delivering to you, and the next member to come online commits your removal from the encrypted group, as removing someone from a topic already does.

4To decide

Who may delete a group for everyone?

Drawn: the person who made it, as the owner deletes a community. The alternative is any member, which is simpler and lets one person end a chat the others wanted.

Does Admin get a Groups page?

Drawn: yes, as metadata only, deleting under the reports-triage permission. Without it, a group whose maker has left the server can never be removed.

oosterparkwijk, now

Wait for Delete for everyone, then remove it from its menu. Deleting its rows by hand on the server stops delivery but leaves it in every browser's list, because nothing tells a device that a plain group is gone.

Rename in this round?

Drawn: yes. It is the cheapest fix for a group that was right but badly named, and the payload exists already.
