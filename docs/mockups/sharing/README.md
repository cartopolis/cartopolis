# Sharing from the app: mockup

A design proposal, not code. One **Share** button in the dock (and in a call's
bar) offers three things to share: **my screen**, **my view in the world**
(what I see, or my avatar), and **video on an object** (a prim). Asked for on
2026-11-19: mockups first, no research or build yet.

What exists today and is redrawn here: sharing the screen inside a call
(More → Share your screen, `docs/notes/calls.md`) and a clip on a prim (edit ▸
Video, `docs/notes/video-on-a-prim.md`). Everything else on the sheet is new
and marked so; "share my view" follows `../workspace/` v1, sheet 2, section D.

## v1

1–3 choosing (what, then with whom), 4–6 my screen, 7–9 my view or avatar,
10–12 video on an object, then the open questions.
