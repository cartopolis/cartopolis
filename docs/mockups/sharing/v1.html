12On the board, for everyone near

Whoever stands near sees and hears it, in step. The pill shows how many are near, and Pause and Stop stay in reach.

Open

  • A live screen on a board is public: anyone within 60 m sees it, unencrypted to the server. Ask once more before starting (“Everyone here will see your screen”)?
  • Who may put a live picture on an object: only its owner, as with clips, or anyone the owner allows (a community's board)?
  • “As a video” for viewers without 3D: keep it, or send only the view and leave the browser to draw it?
  • A phone plays one video at a time today; a live board and a call's stage together may be one too many.
