Communities v2: finding, joining, moderating

Builds on v1 (the list, a community, a topic). Added after review: people find and join communities themselves; a community can ask a moderator to confirm each newcomer first; and a community can be edited after it starts, including who its moderators are. Everything here is new.

Three ways in

Open: anyone who finds it joins. Asks first: a moderator confirms each request. Invite only: not joinable, only added by a moderator.

Listed or not

A listed community shows in Find communities with its name, description and size. An unlisted one is reached only by being added, or by its link.

Three roles

Owner: started it, can delete it or hand it over. Moderators: confirm requests, add and remove people, edit it. Members: talk, and start topics if allowed.

What the server sees

A listed community's name, description, topic names and size are public on this server. What is said in it stays end-to-end encrypted, as in every topic.

NPeople and moderators

  • Who runs it sits on top. ⋯ on anyone: make or stop being moderator, message them, remove them.
  • Moderators can't remove the owner or each other; only the owner can. The owner can hand over ownership, and must before leaving.
  • Removing someone takes them out of every topic; "and block" also stops them asking again.

Open questions

  • Should moderators be able to appoint other moderators, or only the owner (as drawn)?
  • Removing someone takes them out of the encrypted topics, but what they already read stays on their device. That is true of any chat and worth saying in the remove dialog.
  • Can a moderator delete somebody else's message? Possible (every device hides it), but it is a request the server can't enforce on a modified app.
