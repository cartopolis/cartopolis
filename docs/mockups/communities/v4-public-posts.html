Communities v4: public posts, with a place, on the fediverse

A community gets a second, public half beside its encrypted topics: Posts. A member writes a post, it is pinned where it was written, and it goes out over ActivityPub, so people on Mastodon and Lemmy can follow the community, reply, and post to it themselves. The community can also follow fediverse accounts and Lemmy communities and show what they post. Everything on this sheet is new. Topics are unchanged: encrypted, members only, never on the fediverse.

HModerating what comes from outside

  • The same menu as v3, for a fediverse author: hide the post, block that address from the community, and pass the report to its own server.
  • Blocking a whole server is the server admin's, and applies to every community.
  • The word filter that guards public chat is applied to incoming posts too.

What changes, and what doesn't

  • Topics stay encrypted and members-only, and never go over ActivityPub. Posts are the public half, and the server stores them readable, as it must to send them out.
  • A post is a point: the place, the camera view, text and a picture, already stored in the fediverse's own format since the chat work. No conversion.
  • Nobody's real location is used. A post's place is where you aimed the camera, not where your phone is.
  • Nothing live goes over ActivityPub: avatars, voice, Here and location sharing stay on Cartopolis's own connection.
  • The "every area is a community" idea (chat-world v5) stays set aside: places get posts through communities that have a place.

How it would be built

  • The simulator becomes a small fediverse server: a group address per community with posts switched on, a person address per account that posts, /.well-known/webfinger so @oosterpoort@cartopolis.org can be found, and an inbox and outbox. Caddy routes them, like /api.
  • Lemmy's own library, activitypub_federation, does the signing, address lookup and fetching, and works with axum, which the simulator's web side uses. To be checked before relying on it.
  • Edits and deletes travel both ways, which is why following is better than polling Mastodon's API: a deleted post disappears here too.
  • Pictures from outside go through a proxy, so a remote server never sees a Cartopolis user's IP address.
  • Order: posts inside Cartopolis first (A–D, G, H), then sending them out (E, F), then following sources (G's last part).

Open questions

  • Posting as yourself. Drawn: a post comes from @sanne@cartopolis.org and the community boosts it, which is how Lemmy works and gives replies someone to answer. It means every account that posts becomes findable on the fediverse. The other way is posting as the community, which hides the author outside Cartopolis.
  • Default for outside posts: drawn as shown straight away, with moderators hiding afterwards.
  • Addresses are permanent. @oosterpoort cannot be renamed once it has followers without breaking them, so a community's address is chosen when posts are switched on, separately from its display name.
